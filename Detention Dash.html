Detention Dash

Grab the permission slips. Pass the pop quizzes. Get out before Ms. Ledger signs you up for detention.

Who's playing

How to play

Permission slips are hidden in classrooms. Each one comes with a 3-question pop quiz. Ms. Ledger wakes up after your first slip, and every wrong answer makes her faster.

Collect every slip, then reach a glowing EXIT pad. In Plus mode that takes you down to the next floor, where the snack counter is waiting.

Watch out for the other students and staff. Some help, most don't, and all of them talk.

Computer

WASD move · Mouse look (click to capture) · Shift run · F use item · E grab or swap · 1–7 or wheel pick a slot · Space look back · M map · Esc pause. Everything is rebindable in Settings.

Two players on one screen

Player 1 uses WASD and the mouse. Player 2 uses the arrow keys to walk and turn, comma and period to strafe, right Shift to run, Enter to use an item, the quote key to grab, and brackets to switch items. Gamepads work too: plug one in for Player 2, or two for both. On a phone or tablet each half of the screen gets its own stick and buttons.

Bots

Bot classmates hunt for slips, take their own pop quizzes, use items when Ms. Ledger gets close, and run for the exit. Their wrong answers make her faster too. In team games a catch sends someone to detention for 10 seconds instead of ending the run, until the team runs out of chances.

Phone

Left thumb moves, right thumb looks. Buttons for run, use, grab, look back and map. Tap a slot to select it.
