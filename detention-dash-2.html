How to play

Permission slips are hidden around the campus, indoors and out. Each one comes with a pop quiz. Ms. Ledger wakes up when the first slip is turned in, and every wrong answer makes her angrier and faster.

Collect every slip, then reach a glowing EXIT gate in the fence. Plus mode then moves you to the next period of the day.

Staying alive

She sees in a cone and hears footsteps. Running is loud, walking is quieter, crouching is silent. Hide in SPARE lockers, bathroom stalls or dumpsters, but if she saw you go in, she will check. Buy items at Gourda's counter in the cafeteria, or hold the button to steal one. Ask the Advisor for hints. Collect lore pages to learn what happened in 1989.

Teams

When you play with others, getting caught sends you to the detention room. A teammate frees you by holding interact at the DETENTION door. Three catches and you are expelled for the run, and you can spectate. Bots only know about slips they have seen, and they can be caught too. Press the order key to tell them to follow you, split up, distract her or rescue.

Computer

WASD move, mouse look (click to capture), Shift run, C crouch, E interact / buy / hide / rescue, F use item, R flashlight, Q bot orders, 1–7 or wheel pick a slot, Space look back, M map, Esc pause. Player 2: arrows, comma/period strafe, right Shift run, Enter use, ' interact. Gamepads work. Everything is rebindable.

Phone

Left thumb moves, right thumb looks. Buttons for run, crouch, use, interact, flashlight, map and orders. Tap a slot to select it, tap again to use it.
